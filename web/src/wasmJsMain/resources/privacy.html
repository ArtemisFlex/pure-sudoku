PureGames Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

PureGames is made by BCS Gaming and owned by Bratton Creative Solutions, LLC ("we", "us").

In short: PureGames has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your games, scores and settings stay on your device. The only thing that ever leaves it is the "Play a friend online" feature, which passes your moves to your friend and forgets them within two hours.

What stays on your device

Saved games, statistics, leaderboards, favourites and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. If you turn on backups, the app writes a backup file to a folder you choose; Android's own device backup may also copy app data to your Google account under Google's terms. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device.

Play a friend online

Everything in PureGames works offline except this optional feature. When you start or join an online game:

  • Our room service at puregames.app hands out a four-letter room code and passes the connection details both devices need to find each other. Rooms expire after 15 minutes if nobody joins; games expire after 2 hours.
  • The two devices then connect directly. To do that they use public STUN servers run by Google and Cloudflare, which see your device's IP address, as any internet connection does.
  • If a direct link can't be made, your moves are passed through our room service instead and deleted when the game expires.

We don't ask for your name, email, location or any account, and we don't keep logs that tie games to people. Our hosting provider (Vercel) and storage provider (Upstash) process this short-lived data on our behalf and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics libraries.
  • No in-app purchases. Chips and coins in games like Blackjack are play money with no real-world value and can't be bought or cashed out.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

PureGames includes early-learning games for children aged 4 to 10. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Online play only connects two people who share a room code with each other; there is no chat, profile or public matchmaking.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
