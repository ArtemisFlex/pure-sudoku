## Open

### Flashcard learning games for ages 4-10
A new set of flashcard-style learning games: math, words, sentences for reading practice, and more.
- Levels from Easy (age 4) through Extreme (age 10).
- Math from counting and basic add/subtract/multiply/divide up to long division and simple algebra.
- Math problems have an (i) button that shows each step of working the problem, so kids learn how.
