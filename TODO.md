## Open

### New game: a GPS memory driving game (roguelike, endless)
Train memory, recall and sense of direction so players stop relying on GPS.

- **Look:** drawn in code (SVG/vector), styled like Google Maps; light or dark map following the app's theme.
- **City:** one set, huge city layout with street names, multiple routes between places, traffic lights,
  stop signs, dead ends, one-way streets, light timings and other quirks to learn. Stores, restaurants,
  landmarks and customers' homes in fixed places, so they can be learned and remembered.
- **Play:** you're a delivery driver going from A to B (and later several stops), choosing the best route,
  then finding your way back home. Earn coins per delivery.
- **Shrinking help:** turn-by-turn GPS at first → street names and a list of turns → only the destination's
  name, from memory. Less of the route is revealed and routes get longer as you progress. In harder
  challenges, little or no instruction (including the trip home).
- **Coins:** buy car skins and colours, and hints.
- **Structure:** unlike the other games, a roguelike with no end and no difficulty levels: one mode whose city
  keeps expanding and whose difficulty and scenarios escalate dynamically as you play and improve. Three save slots.
- **Goal:** over time players genuinely learn the city: street names, where customers live, where places are.
